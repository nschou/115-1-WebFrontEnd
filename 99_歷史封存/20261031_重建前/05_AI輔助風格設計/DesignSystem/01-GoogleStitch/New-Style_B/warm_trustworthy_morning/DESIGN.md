## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the sensory warmth, clarity, and dependable craft of an artisan morning bakery and cafe. It rejects the cold, sterile tropes of corporate tech dashboards in favor of an environment defined by hospitality, human presence, and understated groundedness.

The visual style blends **Tactile Minimalism** with **Organic Modernity**. Surfaces evoke raw linen, flour-dusted marble, and unglazed ceramic. Interfaces are open, breathable, and deeply calming, designed to reduce decision fatigue and morning rush anxiety.

Key brand pillars:
- **Hospitality First:** Interactions greet the user with quiet confidence and warmth. Tone and framing prioritize reassurance over urgency.
- **Earthy Precision:** Elements balance hand-hewn softness with clear, disciplined structural hierarchy.
- **Natural Tactility:** Form controls, cards, and interactive elements possess an organic, touchable presence rooted in physical materials rather than digital sheen.

## Colors

The palette is derived from natural baking materials, kiln-fired pottery, and morning flora. No stark monochrome whites or harsh pure blacks are permitted.

- **Primary (`#B85332` - Brick Terracotta):** Used intentionally for primary calls-to-action, key milestones, and focal reassurance badges. Conveys baked crust, earthenware, and human energy without aggressive neon alarm.
- **Secondary (`#7C8B7A` - Soft Sage):** Evokes fresh herbs and calm counterbalance. Used for secondary actions, subtle confirmation cues, and restorative accents.
- **Tertiary (`#D99B62` - Toasted Oats / Warm Amber):** Reserved for highlights, soft progress indications, and ambient badge highlights.
- **Neutral Canvas & Tiers:**
  - Base Canvas: Warm Linen (`#FDFBF7`)
  - Elevated Container / Cards: Soft Oatmilk (`#FAF6F0`)
  - Subdued Well / Inset: Milled Flour (`#F4EFEA`)
- **Typography & Structural Neutrals:**
  - Ink Primary: Deep Roasted Espresso (`#2D2522`) ensures full WCAG AAA contrast without digital harshness.
  - Ink Secondary: Warm Umber (`#5C504A`) for supporting content and labels.
  - Ink Muted: Stone Ochre (`#8C7E77`) for disabled states and tertiary hints.
  - Border & Dividers: Faint Husk (`#E8DFD5`), soft and organic.

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** governs all hierarchy levels. Its balanced geometric foundations combined with rounded, human-friendly apertures deliver a welcoming presence without sacrificing institutional legibility or alignment integrity.

### Typesetting Rules
- **Headlines:** Use tighter line spacing and slight negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to maintain editorial cohesion and confidence.
- **Body Copy:** Generous line heights (`1.6` to `1.65` ratio) prevent cognitive crowding, allowing quick scanning during busy morning workflows.
- **Case Formatting:** Sentence case is mandatory across headings, actions, and pill badges. Avoid aggressive full-uppercase strings except for very small contextual tags (`label-sm`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a **Generous Fluid Grid** anchored by open margins and reassuring vertical breathing room. The interface avoids dense data grids, preferring modular, card-based stacks that give each piece of content room to settle.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column layout, max content width of `1200px`, centered with `2.5rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout, `2rem` margins, `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `1.25rem` outer canvas padding, `1rem` gutters. Stacks collapse gracefully into clean single-column or paired-card rhythms.

### Vertical Rhythm
- Stack cards and sections with a minimum gap of `space-md` on mobile and `space-lg` on desktop.
- Header groups maintain `space-sm` between eyebrow, title, and supporting subtitle to preserve immediate semantic association.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system is organic and atmospheric, relying on warm tonal layering combined with amber-tinted diffused shadows. Cold gray dropped drop-shadows are prohibited.

### Tonal Stratification
1. **Base Plane:** Canvas background set to `#FDFBF7`.
2. **Resting Surfaces (Cards, Modules):** `#FAF6F0` framed by a 1px soft border in `#E8DFD5`.
3. **Elevated Panels (Sheets, Flyouts, Menus):** Pure warm white `#FFFFFF` layered over the linen base.

### Ambient Warm Shadow System
- **Level 1 (Cards, Rest State):** `0px 2px 8px rgba(74, 52, 41, 0.04), 0px 1px 2px rgba(74, 52, 41, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover, Interactive Cards):** `0px 8px 20px rgba(74, 52, 41, 0.06), 0px 2px 6px rgba(74, 52, 41, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays):** `0px 16px 36px rgba(74, 52, 41, 0.08), 0px 4px 12px rgba(74, 52, 41, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The geometric vocabulary prioritizes organic comfort through generous radii that evoke rounded loaves, smooth ceramic cups, and soft paper stationery. 

- **Containers & Cards:** Styled with `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) to soften visual perimeters while remaining structured.
- **Modal Dialogs & Feature Blocks:** Styled with `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`).
- **Interactive Badges, Pills & Controls:** Styled with full pill radii (`9999px`) to create tangible, friendly targets that invite interaction.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#B85332` with `#FFFFFF` text. Generous internal padding (`0.875rem 1.75rem`), pill-shaped (`9999px`). Subdued warm shadow on rest; scales down slightly (`0.98`) with darkened terracotta (`#A14527`) on active press.
- **Secondary / Soft:** Surface tinted in `#FAF6F0`, border 1px `#E8DFD5`, text in `#2D2522`. Hover transitions subtly to `#F4EFEA`.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background, text in `#5C504A`, hover to soft oat wash (`rgba(184, 83, 50, 0.06)`).

### Badges & Pill Tags
- Fully rounded (`9999px`), padding `0.375rem 0.875rem`.
- **Informative:** Light oat tone (`#F4EFEA`) with `#5C504A` text.
- **Success / Fresh:** Soft sage tint (`rgba(124, 139, 122, 0.15)`) with `#4E5C4C` text accompanied by a fine-line organic checkmark.
- **Warm Reassurance:** Terracotta wash (`rgba(184, 83, 50, 0.1)`) with `#B85332` text.

### Cards
- Resting surface in `#FAF6F0` bordered by `1px solid #E8DFD5` with Level 1 warm shadow.
- Inner padding ranges from `1.25rem` on mobile to `1.75rem` on desktop.
- Interactive cards elevate smoothly to Level 2 shadow with an outline tint shift to `#D9CBBF`.

### Form Inputs & Selects
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#E8DFD5`, radius `0.75rem` (12px), text in `#2D2522`.
- Focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(184, 83, 50, 0.2)` with a border transition to `#B85332`.
- Placeholder text in `#8C7E77`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `1.25rem` (20px). Radios are fully circular; checkboxes use `0.375rem` (6px) rounded corners.
- Default border `1.5px solid #8C7E77` over `#FAF6F0`.
- Selected state fills with `#B85332` with a crisp, centered white checkmark or inner dot.

### Greeting Headers & Status Banners
- Dedicated card module featuring warm baked gradients (`#FAF6F0` transitioning imperceptibly to `#F4EFEA`).
- Accompanied by humanized conversational timestamps (e.g., "Freshly prepared this morning").
- No generic emojis; all status indicators use clean, warm line iconography crafted at 1.5px stroke width.
